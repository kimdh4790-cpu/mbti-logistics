FAQ

Common questions

Does MailMind read all my emails? +
No. MailMind only reads the email you currently have open, and only when you click "Generate." It never scans your inbox in the background.
Which languages are supported? +
MailMind supports 17 languages including Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic, Vietnamese, Thai, Indonesian, Italian, Turkish, Dutch, Polish, and Hindi. Any combination works — Japanese to Portuguese, Arabic to Korean, anything.
What if I don't speak the language at all? +
That's exactly what MailMind is for. The Smart Reply feature reads the email, shows you a summary in your language, then writes a perfect reply in the sender's language. You never need to understand the original email.
What AI model does it use? +
MailMind uses Claude by Anthropic — one of the most capable AI models available. This is why replies feel natural, culturally appropriate, and not "AI-generated."
Is my email data safe? +
Yes. Email content is processed in real-time and immediately discarded after generating your reply. We never store, share, or use your emails to train AI models.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes, cancel anytime with one click. No hidden fees, no lock-in, no questions asked.
